Consulting

Statistical expert and technical consultant for defense attorneys, nonprofits, and local governments.

I help legal and justice organizations turn messy administrative data into clear, defensible evidence, and I explain it in terms that judges, juries, and policymakers can follow.

Expert Witness — California Racial Justice Act

I provide statistical analysis and expert testimony for California Racial Justice Act claims under Penal Code § 745(a)(3) and (a)(4).

I have worked on seven RJA claims across five counties, providing expert statistical analysis for a county public defender's office, an independent defense attorney, a law school racial justice clinic, and a nonprofit legal organization. Courts in Kern and Los Angeles Counties have approved funding for my services as a defense expert.

In collaboration with the Office of the State Public Defender, I led technical development of the RJA Disparity Screener, a website that generates court-ready reports on post-conviction racial disparities.

If you are working on an RJA case and want to know whether a statistical analysis is feasible, I'm happy to talk it through. Get in touch.

Mathematical Modeling and Large Datasets

My background is in weather and climate modeling, where I worked with large, complex datasets and model output. I bring that experience to legal, nonprofit, and local government projects: working with large datasets and building new mathematical models of systems such as traffic flow when existing tools don't fit the question.

Data Acquisition and Structuring

Getting usable data is often the hardest part. I coordinate public records requests, clean and link administrative datasets, and design human-validated AI pipelines that turn documents such as police reports into structured data ready for statistical analysis.

Interactive Websites and Dashboards

I design and build public-facing tools and dashboards that make complex data accessible. Past work includes the Opportunity Youth Index (video walkthrough), which maps youth disconnection, youth arrests, and parental incarceration across U.S. communities.
