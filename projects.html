Selected Projects

  • California Racial Justice Act — Statistical Expert Witness
    Expert witness work · Ongoing

    Statistical analysis supporting §745(a)(3) and (a)(4) claims under the California Racial Justice Act. Work includes analysis of charging, conviction, and sentencing disparities for use in evidentiary hearings. Clients include public defenders, nonprofit legal organizations, and individual attorneys.

  • Post-Conviction Racial Disparity Tool
    Office of the State Public Defender, California

    Designed and built a public-facing website that generates court-ready documents showing post-conviction racial disparities. Built to support individuals using pro per materials in post-conviction proceedings.

  • State Investment Tracker — Death Penalty Abolition
    Nonprofit client · Ongoing

    Built a private dashboard tracking state-level government investment relevant to death penalty abolition strategy. Automated pipeline ingests daily Google News RSS feeds, structures them using the OpenAI API, and surfaces results in dynamically updating tables.

  • Fair Chance Hiring: Policy and Employer Practice
    Commissioned research · 2023

    Led a research team examining the association between state fair chance hiring policies and employer behavior. Constructed an original index of state policy environments across six policy types (including ban-the-box and licensing restrictions) and scraped fair chance job postings from Indeed across relevant industries. Findings published in The Prison Journal.

  • Opportunity Youth Index
    Dashboard · 2022

    Created an interactive dashboard displaying youth disconnection, youth arrests, and parental incarceration rates by U.S. Census PUMA. Combined these indicators into a composite Opportunity Youth Index to support community-level planning and advocacy.
